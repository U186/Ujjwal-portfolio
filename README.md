## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📊 [Power BI – E-Commerce Sales & Customer Dashboard](https://github.com/U186/powerbi-ecommerce-dashboard)
> *Tools: Power BI | Domain: E-Commerce Analytics*

A **5-page interactive dashboard** analyzing 50K+ transactions across revenue, customer segmentation, churn, and returns.

**Key Insights Found:**
- Credit Card drives 40%+ of all revenue — recommended exclusive discounts to boost AOV
- 46+ age group is the highest revenue segment — ideal for premium product targeting
- Churned customers (~20% of base) concentrated in Clothing & Books — flagged for re-engagement campaigns
- Revenue drops sharply post-June — identified need for Q3 promotional strategy
