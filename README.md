## 📌 Overview

This folder contains work completed during my **3-month Digital Marketing Internship at Quazze Software** (March – May 2025).

My role involved creating social media content, supporting digital campaigns, and assisting with website design tasks.

---

## 💼 Role & Responsibilities

**Position:** Digital Marketing Intern
**Company:** Quazze Software
**Duration:** March 2025 – May 2025

### What I Did:
- **Designed 7 social media creatives** using Canva for the brand's Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn presence
- **Followed a weekly content calendar** — planned and structured posts for consistent brand communication
- **Conducted competitor and market research** to support content strategy and messaging decisions
- **Supported email marketing campaigns** by assisting in setup and monitoring delivery workflows
- **Assisted with website design tasks** using Webflow — contributed to layout and basic design elements
- **Collaborated with the team** on content structuring, scheduling, and digital workflow management
